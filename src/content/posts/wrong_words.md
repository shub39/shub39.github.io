# Why am I writing this?
I encounter these words from time to time and It just grinds my gears every time I see them. I'm just listing them here
to keep track of them and share with others easily. Just to see if I'm the only one who feels this way. I'm planning to
make this a comprehensive list ordered in alphabetical order with usages and the best explainations to why I don't like
them.

---

## Content 

- The *content* of this channel is amazing.
- He is a talented *content* creator.

**Content** as in refering to the posts/videos/media of a person/organisation in the internet. There's something very 
dehumanizing about this word. It is such a broad word and can be used to refer to any type of media. All types of 
online media can be considered someone's *content*.

A priest talking about religion on the internet is *content*. A social media influencer posting beautiful travel photos
is *content*. When someone refers to their media as *content*, it suddenly snaps all my interest away from the person
and into the media itself. The word *content* just gives of a vibe of industrial monotonous repetition. 
Paired with the word *consumption* and it becomes even more repulsive. What do you mean I *consume content*? I consume
food too. And it makes sense there. For some reason, I associate consumption with necessity. And I don't think 
online media is a necessity. 

Phrases like "Watching videos" and "Scrolling reels" are much better alternatives to *content consumption*.
Instead of saying "I like the content the make", I prefer to say "I like the videos/pictures/blogs they post"
